Meet Nikolai

Tools for a consciously
chosen life.

I’m Nikolai, founder of Coherence Nikolai and creator of these apps.

I make these little apps, comics and stories as signals of a Golden Age of humanity: a world defined by love, where we move beyond self-serving division towards shared care.

If they reach even one person and make that possibility feel a little closer, they have a place here.

Coherence Nikolai is a collection of private apps for meditation practice, emotional steadiness, study support, and self-inquiry.

No ads. No social feed. No account where possible. The aim is simple: useful support when you need it.
