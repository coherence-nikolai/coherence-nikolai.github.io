A note from Nikolai

Why I make these things.

I make these little apps, comics and stories as signals of a Golden Age of humanity: a world defined by love, where we move beyond self-serving division towards shared care.

If they reach even one person and make that possibility feel a little closer, they have a place here.

Not for me. For all.
One choice at a time.

In my own voice

An optional introduction. Listen when you wish.
