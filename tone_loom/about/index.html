About the instrument

Tone Loom

A playable wheel for weaving paths of tone and light.

Move around a tone wheel, listen to the path it makes and combine what you find. Tone Loom is an exploratory instrument: it offers structures to play with, not a right sequence to complete.

Open Tone Loom →All apps

Play

Run a finger or pointer around a wheel and hear the tones in its chosen order.

Weave

Record a short path, combine it with a visual pattern and replay the sequence.

Keep locally

Saved wheels and spell paths remain part of this browser experience unless you choose to export them.
